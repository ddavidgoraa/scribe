Markdown editor for macOS

Less interface,
more writing.

Scribe is a calm place to write. Formatting appears as you type, math is typeset on the page, and your notes stay plain Markdown files in a folder you choose.

Features

Everything you need to write. Nothing in the way.

Live preview

Markdown symbols like ** and # step aside until your cursor touches them. No preview pane.

Math, typeset

Write $E = mc^2$ or a $$ block and see the formula, with a live preview while you edit.

Beautiful type

Sans, serif or mono in a centered column of 64, 72 or 80 characters. Light, dark or automatic.

Focus & typewriter

Fade everything but the paragraph you're writing, and keep the current line in the middle of the screen.

Find anything

Open notes by name with ⌘⇧O; search every note with ⌘⇧F — case and accents don't matter.

Pictures & print

Paste or drag in images and see them inline. Print any note — or save a PDF — as a clean, typeset document.

Your notes, your Mac

Private by design.

  • Offline. No accounts, analytics, crash reports or update checks. Images from the web are never downloaded.
  • Sandboxed. Scribe runs in the macOS App Sandbox and can only open the notes folder you choose.
  • Plain files. Every note is a .md file you can open anywhere, sync with iCloud or keep in git.
  • Open source. Read the code, build it yourself, and verify every release. How we keep it safe →

Start writing.

Download Scribe, choose a folder, press ⌘N.
